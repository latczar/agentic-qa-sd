# Laptop very slow after a system update

A laptop that has become sluggish immediately after an update is usually busy
rather than broken. Post-update indexing and driver reinstallation can occupy
the disk for up to two hours on older hardware.

Before treating it as a fault:

1. Check disk activity. Sustained near-100% disk with low CPU points at
   indexing, which finishes on its own.
2. Confirm the update actually completed rather than waiting on a restart.
3. Leave the machine powered on and connected for two hours.

If it is still slow after a full restart and two hours of idle time, collect the
model, the update version, and how long the slowdown has lasted, then escalate to
the desktop team. Reimaging is not an appropriate first response.
