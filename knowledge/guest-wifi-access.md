# Wi-Fi access for visitors

Guest Wi-Fi is self-service. Visitors connect to the guest network, enter their
mobile number, and receive a code by text valid for 24 hours. No ticket is
needed and reception does not hold a shared password.

The guest network reaches the internet only. Visitors cannot see internal
systems, printers or shared drives from it, which is deliberate.

Visitors staying longer than a week, or needing to present to a meeting room
screen, need a sponsored account instead. Sponsorship is requested by their host
and lasts up to 30 days.

Never connect a visitor device to the corporate network, and never share your
own credentials with them.
