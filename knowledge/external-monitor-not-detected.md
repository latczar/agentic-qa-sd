# External monitor not detected or flickering

Start by reseating both ends of the video cable. Flickering and intermittent
detection are far more often a loose or failing cable than a fault in the
laptop or the screen.

If the monitor is connected through a dock, connect it directly to the laptop
to establish which of the three is at fault. A dock that worked yesterday and
not today usually needs a firmware update from the company portal.

Windows sometimes remembers a stale display arrangement after a dock change.
Pressing the display shortcut and choosing extend again forces a redetect,
which resolves the case where the monitor has power but shows nothing.

High refresh rate monitors over a single cable can exceed what the dock
supports, which shows up as flickering under load rather than a clean failure.
