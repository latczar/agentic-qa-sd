# Email stops syncing on mobile devices

Mail that has stopped arriving on a phone while still arriving in the desktop
client is nearly always a device-side problem, not a mailbox one.

Check first:

1. Confirm mail is arriving in the web client. If it is not, the problem is the
   mailbox and this article does not apply.
2. Check the device has a working data or wifi connection.
3. Check whether the device has been marked non-compliant by device management -
   a non-compliant device has its mail access withdrawn automatically and shows
   exactly this symptom.

If the device is compliant and online, remove the mail account from the device
and add it again. Do not delete the mailbox profile on the desktop client as
part of this - it is unrelated and risks losing locally cached folders.
