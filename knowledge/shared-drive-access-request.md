# Requesting access to a shared drive or folder

Access to shared drives is granted by the team that owns the data, not by the
service desk. Raising a ticket with us adds a step rather than removing one.

Find the owning team from the folder properties, or from the access request
page which lists an owner for every shared location. Submit the request there
and the owner is notified directly.

Approved access usually applies within fifteen minutes, but a session that is
already signed in keeps the old permissions until you sign out and back in.

Requests for folders containing payroll, HR or customer personal data need a
second approver and are reviewed weekly rather than immediately.
