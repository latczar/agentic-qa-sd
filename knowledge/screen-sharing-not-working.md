# Cannot share screen in a meeting

On a rebuilt or newly issued laptop, screen sharing usually fails because the
operating system screen recording permission has never been granted to the
meeting client. The client shows a blank or frozen share rather than an error,
which makes this hard to guess at.

Grant the permission under privacy settings, then quit and reopen the meeting
client completely - it reads the permission only at startup, so leaving it
running gives the impression the change did nothing.

Sharing a single window rather than the whole screen fails more often than it
works when the window is minimised or on a disconnected monitor.

If the share works but looks slow or blocky to others, that is bandwidth rather
than permissions, and turning off your camera while presenting helps most.
