# Laptop will not charge

Most charging failures are the cable or the dock rather than the laptop. Try
the charger directly in a wall socket first, bypassing any dock or monitor that
normally supplies power.

USB-C charging is directional in practice: some ports on some models accept
power and others do not. Try each port before concluding the charger has died.

If the light comes on but the battery percentage does not move, and the machine
runs only while plugged in, the battery has failed rather than the charger.
Battery health is visible in the device support app, and anything below 50% of
design capacity is replaced.

Docks that stop supplying power after an update usually need a firmware update,
pushed through the company portal.
