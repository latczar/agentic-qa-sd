# Resetting your password with self-service

Password resets no longer need a service desk ticket. The self-service portal
handles them directly, provided the account has at least one verification method
registered.

To reset:

1. Go to the identity portal and choose "Forgotten password".
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Approve the prompt on your registered phone, or enter the code sent by SMS.
4. Choose a new password meeting the complexity rules below.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of the last five.
Changes take up to five minutes to reach all systems, so a brief window where
the old password still works on some services is expected, not a fault.

Accounts with no registered verification method cannot use self-service and must
have identity confirmed by an agent before a manual reset.
