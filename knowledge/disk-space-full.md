# Laptop is out of disk space

Windows begins warning at around 10% free and becomes unstable below 2%, at
which point updates fail silently and applications may refuse to open.

The usual culprits, in the order worth checking: the Downloads folder, locally
cached meeting recordings, previous Windows installation files left behind by a
feature update, and offline copies of large shared folders that were never
meant to sync.

Storage Sense under system settings clears temporary files and previous
installations safely and usually recovers several gigabytes. It does not touch
documents.

Do not clear space by removing anything in the user profile you do not
recognise - some of it is the sync client working state, and deleting it can
cause the data loss you were trying to avoid.
