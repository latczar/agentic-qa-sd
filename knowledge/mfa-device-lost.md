# Lost or replaced phone with MFA set up

When the phone holding your authenticator app is lost, replaced or wiped, the
codes on it are gone for good. They cannot be recovered or transferred after
the fact, so the registration has to be reset by an agent.

Before contacting the service desk, check whether a second method is still
registered. Many accounts have both an authenticator app and an SMS number, and
if the number is still yours you can sign in with that and re-register the app
yourself from the security settings page.

If no working method remains, an agent must verify your identity before
resetting MFA. Verification is by video call with photo ID, not over email,
because resetting MFA for the wrong person hands over the account.

Once reset, you have 24 hours to register a new method before the account is
blocked from signing in again.
