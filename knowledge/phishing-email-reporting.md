# Reporting a suspicious or phishing email

Use the Report Phishing button in the mail client rather than forwarding the
message. Forwarding strips the headers security needs and puts a live link in
front of another person.

The button moves the message out of your mailbox and sends it for analysis. You
will not usually get a reply - no reply means it was handled, not ignored.

If you have already clicked a link or entered credentials, treat it as urgent.
Change your password immediately from a different device and tell the service
desk straight away, saying what you entered and roughly when. Nobody is in
trouble for reporting quickly, and speed limits the damage.

Do not delete the message before reporting it.
